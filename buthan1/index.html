Paro Taktsang — The Tiger's Nest

Clinging to a cliff 900 meters above the Paro valley, this monastery marks where Guru Rinpoche is said to have flown on a tigress's back.

1
Location— Paro Valley, western Bhutan
2
Elevation— 3,120 m (10,240 ft)
3
Built— 1692, around a sacred cave
4
Legend— Guru Rinpoche arrived on a flying tigress
