Mount Kailash — The Unclimbed Summit

Rising 6,638 meters over western Tibet, Kailash is revered by four faiths — and has never been confirmed climbed.

1
Location— western Tibet, by Lake Manasarovar
2
Height— 6,638 m (21,778 ft)
3
Sacred to— Buddhists, Hindus, Jains & Bon
4
Ascent— considered forbidden; summit unclimbed
